# AI and Model Training Policy

Ontologica OS is visible for review, citation, discussion, and collaboration inquiry. It is not open source and does not grant model-training, benchmark, implementation, production, derivative-work, or dataset-construction rights.

## No Permission Granted

Without prior written agreement, no permission is granted to use this repository or its contents for:

- training artificial intelligence systems
- fine-tuning or distillation
- embedding or retrieval-augmentation corpora
- dataset construction
- synthetic dataset generation
- benchmark construction or benchmark evaluation
- agent evaluation suites
- automated code generation systems
- model-derived reimplementation
- architecture reconstruction
- product, service, internal-tool, or hosted-system development

## Research or Evaluation Requests

Research, evaluation, benchmark, teaching, or commercial use may be considered only by separate written agreement.

Use the collaboration path in [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md). Do not assume permission from public visibility, citation, discussion, forks, issues, or pull requests.

## Noncanonical Examples

Toy examples, toy schemas, noncanonical manifests, and noncanonical receipts in this repository are included to explain vocabulary and boundaries only. They are not training data, benchmark data, production examples, or implementation grants.
