# Doctrine

Ontologica OS is governed by these public rules:

1. The public reference is noncanonical.
2. Language models propose; they do not crown truth.
3. Deterministic kernels stabilize proposals into replayable transforms.
4. SHA manifests identify artifacts; they do not replace review.
5. Drift guards detect silent change.
6. Receipts preserve evidence.
7. Promotion gates decide whether candidate work is held, denied, or promoted inside an authorized system.

## Core invariant

Candidate work is not durable truth.

A candidate may be useful, well-formed, deterministic, and receipt-backed while still remaining unpromoted.
