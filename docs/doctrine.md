# Doctrine

Ontologica OS is a source-visible public lens for a class of drift-guarded, receipt-backed, mathematically bounded agentic architectures.

## Core doctrine

1. The canonical system remains private.
2. The public lens has no authority.
3. Language models propose; they do not crown truth.
4. Mathematical kernels stabilize cognition into replayable transforms.
5. SHA manifests identify artifacts; they do not replace review.
6. Drift guards detect silent change.
7. Receipts provide evidence.
8. Promotion gates decide durable truth.
9. Actuation remains separate and denied by default.

## Governing sentence

Ontologica OS publishes the grammar of the architecture, not the machinery of the architecture.

## Public abstraction

LLM cognition may classify, propose, summarize, or route. Deterministic kernels compute. Manifests identify. Drift guards compare. Receipts record evidence. Promotion gates decide whether a candidate may become durable state.
