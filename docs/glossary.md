# Glossary

This glossary defines public Ontologica OS vocabulary. The definitions are noncanonical and explanatory only.

## Agentic Cognition

The proposal, classification, routing, critique, or summarization behavior performed by language models or other cognitive workers. Agentic cognition may produce candidates, not durable truth.

## Tessera

The smallest public symbolic unit in the explanatory vocabulary. In this repository, tesserae are synthetic examples only.

## Kernel

A bounded worker contract with Law, Work, and Proof. A kernel limits what a worker may read, propose, compute, validate, or claim.

## Law

The part of a kernel contract that states forbidden actions, authority ceilings, stop conditions, and escalation rules.

## Work

The part of a kernel contract that states allowed inputs, outputs, files, tools, computations, and review surfaces.

## Proof

The part of a kernel contract that states what receipt, manifest, test, replay, validation, or review bundle is required before progress can be claimed.

## Receipt

A structured evidence artifact produced by a deterministic tool, validation pass, replay, test, or review gate. A receipt provides evidence; it does not crown truth by itself.

## Manifest

An identity and lineage record for inputs, outputs, schemas, kernel versions, tool versions, receipts, and authority ceilings.

## SHA Identity

A hash-bound identity marker. A SHA proves identity of content under a hash function; it does not prove truth, safety, authority, or promotion eligibility.

## Drift Guard

A comparison layer that detects silent change across schemas, projections, models, receipts, authority ceilings, manifests, or promotion claims.

## Promotion Gate

The explicit decision point where candidate material is held, reviewed, quarantined, denied, or promoted to a more durable state.

## Candidate-Only

A state indicating that an artifact may be inspected or discussed but must not be treated as durable truth, production logic, canonical proof, or authority-bearing implementation.

## Noncanonical

A label indicating that an artifact explains vocabulary or architecture without defining private truth or granting implementation rights.

## No Backflow

The rule that public artifacts do not modify, substantiate, patch, define, or govern any private canonical repository.

## Authority Ceiling

The maximum authority an artifact, worker, receipt, tool, or kernel may claim. Public examples in this repository are limited to noncanonical, candidate-only authority.

## Durable Truth

A state that private systems may use as promoted truth after review and promotion. Durable truth is not implemented in this public repository.

## Actuation

Any external-world, hardware, runtime, or side-effecting action. Public examples do not grant actuation authority.
