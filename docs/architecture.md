# Architecture

Ontologica OS is a public lens for drift-guarded, receipt-backed agentic cognition.

This architecture map is noncanonical. It explains invariants and vocabulary only; it does not publish private machinery or production authority paths.

## Layer Responsibilities

### LLM Cognition Layer

Language models may classify, summarize, propose, route, or critique candidate material. They do not crown truth, mutate durable state, or gain authority from fluency.

### Kernel Contract Gate

Every worker is bounded by Law, Work, and Proof. A worker that cannot map a task to those three surfaces must stop with an explicit denial or review state.

### Mathematical Kernel

Deterministic mathematical transforms stabilize selected symbolic inputs into replayable projections that can be hashed, compared, and receipt-bound.

The public harness uses simplified toy math and does not reveal production kernels.

### SHA Manifest Layer

Manifests identify inputs, kernel versions, schemas, outputs, receipts, and authority ceilings. A SHA proves identity, not truth.

### Drift Guard

Drift guards compare manifest-bound states. They make silent change visible across schemas, projections, models, receipts, authority ceilings, and promotion claims.

### Receipt Layer

Receipts record evidence from deterministic tools, validation passes, replay, tests, or review gates. Receipts support promotion decisions but do not replace them.

### Promotion Gate

Promotion gates decide whether candidate material remains held, enters review, is quarantined, is denied, or becomes a more durable state.

### Durable Truth

Durable truth is not implemented in this public repository. Public examples are synthetic and noncanonical.

## Noncanonical Rule

The architecture stops at explanation. Nothing in this repository grants authority over private code, private math, private manifests, private receipts, private runtime, private hardware paths, or private release processes.
