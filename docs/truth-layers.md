# Truth Layers

Ontologica OS separates proposed work from durable truth.

## Candidate layer

The candidate layer holds proposals, projections, generated artifacts, review drafts, and experimental outputs.

Candidate work may be useful, reproducible, and receipt-backed without being durable truth.

## Evidence layer

The evidence layer contains manifests, receipts, hashes, replay inputs, and review records.

Evidence supports governance. Evidence does not replace governance.

## Promotion layer

The promotion layer decides whether candidate work is held, denied, quarantined, or promoted.

## Durable truth layer

Durable truth exists only inside an authorized system. Ontologica OS models the pattern without claiming that authority.
